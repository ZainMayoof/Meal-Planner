Berry yogurt bowl

Simple recipe · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 200 g Greek yogurt
  • 100 g mixed berries
  • 30 g granola
  • 1 tsp honey

Method

  1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
  2. Wash the berries and add them on top.
  3. Finish with granola and a drizzle of honey.

Pesto pasta salad

Simple recipe · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 75 g dry pasta
  • 1 tbsp pesto
  • 100 g cherry tomatoes
  • A handful of baby spinach
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to its packet, then drain and cool slightly.
  2. Halve the tomatoes and rinse the spinach.
  3. Toss the pasta with pesto, olive oil, tomatoes and spinach.

Tofu veggie bowl

Simple recipe · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 150 g firm tofu
  • 150 g cooked rice
  • 100 g broccoli
  • 1 small carrot
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Pat the tofu dry and cut it into cubes. Slice the carrot and cut the broccoli into florets.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the tofu until golden.
  3. Add the vegetables with a splash of water and cook until tender. Stir in the soy sauce.
  4. Serve over the cooked rice.
